Research

IEEE Conference Publication

Explainable Deep Learning for Automated Lung Nodule Classification

A. Padmanabhuni — under the guidance of Dr. Senthil Kumar Thangavel

Abstract

An explainable GoogLeNet-based lung nodule classification model trained on the LUNA16 CT dataset using transfer learning. The system integrates Grad-CAM for visual interpretability, localizing model attention within clinically relevant nodule regions, and outperforms conventional CNN and ResNet-50 baselines on both accuracy and inference speed.
